# CCPA / CPRA (California) — Engineering Checklist

> Not legal advice. Applies to for-profit businesses meeting the thresholds: annual revenue above the inflation-adjusted
> limit, buying/selling/sharing personal information of 100,000+ consumers or households, or earning ≥ 50% of revenue from selling or sharing it.

- [ ] Notice at collection: categories, purposes, retention, whether data is sold or shared
- [ ] "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link (if applicable) and **honour Global Privacy Control (GPC)** signals
- [ ] "Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information" (if sensitive PI is used beyond permitted purposes)
- [ ] Rights: know/access, delete, correct, opt out of sale/sharing, limit sensitive PI, non-discrimination
- [ ] Respond to verified requests within 45 days. At least two request methods
- [ ] Service-provider / contractor contracts with the required terms
- [ ] Reasonable security (a breach of unencrypted PI creates private right of action exposure)
- [ ] Opt-in consent to sell or share PI of consumers under 16
- [ ] Data retention schedule disclosed and enforced
- [ ] Risk assessments / cybersecurity audits / automated decision-making rules (CPPA regulations): check current applicability
